Vumeloo Studios · Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026 · Applies to the "vumeloo-publisher" app and the Instagram accounts @vumeloostudios and @youarehere.shorts

What this app does

vumeloo-publisher is an internal tool used only by Vumeloo Studios to publish our own videos as Reels on our own Instagram accounts and to read the comments people leave on our own posts, so we can count the A/B votes in our interactive series and decide what happens in the next episode.

What data we read

  • Our own posts and their like and comment counts.
  • The text of public comments on our own posts, and the number of likes on each comment.

What we do not do

  • We do not store usernames, profile pictures, names or any other profile information of the people who comment. Only the comment text is processed, and only to count votes and summarise ideas.
  • We do not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties, and we do not use it for advertising.
  • We do not send messages to people from this app.

How long we keep it

Comment texts are kept only in an aggregated vote summary for the episode they belong to and are overwritten when the next count runs.

Deleting your data

If you delete your comment on Instagram, it is no longer read. To ask us to remove anything related to you, see the data deletion page or email namistaintv@gmail.com.
